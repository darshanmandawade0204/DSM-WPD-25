About:-

MET Bhujbal Knowledge City, established in 2006, is a private university in Nashik. It is affiliated with SPPU, Pune, and approved by AICTE. It offers many courses, such as BTech, BE, BDes, BPharma, etc., at the UG level and MBA, MPharm, MCA, etc., at the PG level.
